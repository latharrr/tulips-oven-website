About Us

A kitchen, not a factory

Tulip's Oven started in a Delhi kitchen with one oven, a stand mixer, and zero interest in cutting corners.

We still bake that way: everything made after you order, boxed the same day. If a batch doesn't come out right, it doesn't go out at all. It's the kind of baking you'd do for the people you love, just with a few more trays.

  • One oven
  • Zero freezers
  • Small batches
